What the business/project does:
Kavish Snaps provides professional automotive photography for people and businesses selling cars online. The service focuses on creating high-quality, visually appealing photos that present the car at its best and make online listings look more professional.

Primary goal of the website:
Generate leads and bookings from people who need professional photos for a car they are selling.

Private car sellers, car dealers and other businesses selling vehicles online who want better photography for their listings.
